# Prompt changelog: job-application research
Codex, October 11, 2026. Companion to the completed playbook.

## Verdict

Executed with explicit evidence gaps. The original prompt's personalized decisions, dated sources and per-source failure reporting are worth keeping. Its weakest parts are ambiguous evidence classes, missing canonical candidate inputs, a very narrow recency window for stable hiring practices, and an engine contract that can silently expand irrelevant coverage or shrink planned questions.

The improved prompt below governs this run's deliverable. It extends the original research specification with verification, candid process feedback and the authorized agent handoff. It does not supersede the original file or change the master job-search strategy.

## Full revised prompt

You are conducting private job-application research for one candidate. Produce a prioritized playbook the candidate and their job-search agent can use this week, then deliver it to the authorized agent. Execute the work without avoidable planning questions.

Read the complete wrapper, operating-contract and persistence instructions at their canonical URLs. Follow applicable local AGENTS.md and the installed last30days skill. Preserve higher-priority permission and tool boundaries. Read the complete source prompt at:

<path to the research spec and execution prompt>

Outcome: a decision-ready, dated and evidence-classed playbook, clear research gaps, first actions for four role examples, an honest source-coverage report, candid process feedback, and a verified email handoff. Research completion does not mean every question has a proven answer.

Person and constraints:
- <One-paragraph candidate profile: current trajectory, target role families, hard gates (for this run: never position the candidate as a software engineer), compensation floor and how it is measured, location/remote/travel limits, and confidentiality rules.>
- Treat the supplied history as a dated snapshot. Locate the current resume and portfolio before adding claims. Preserve discrepancies instead of resolving them by invention.
- No invented experience, outcomes, credentials, quotations or statistics. Anonymize anything the candidate's public-content rules require.
- Distinguish task criteria from any conflicting standing search criteria. Do not change master filters silently.

Use the supplied role snapshots only to test applicability:
1. <Role snapshot: title, reported terms, the decision to make.>
2. <...one per live role, each with its gate: unposted pay, unverified remote terms, wrong-role prep materials, and so on.>

Do not turn the assignment into employer research, a new job-sourcing run, applications, or outreach sends. Do not infer continuing vacancy availability. The only send authorized in this request is the final agent research handoff.

Research all seven threads:
- Cover letters: actual reviewer use, structure, length and what gets scanned. Decide conditional use and a practical template.
- Portfolio: outcomes versus artifacts, cases, videos, review time and presentation format for relevant role families. Decide how to package existing proof before recommending a new website.
- Follow-up: timing, content, channel, frequency and negative reactions. Decide a restrained sequence and distinguish application from interview follow-up.
- Direct manager outreach: outcomes, verified manager identification, professional channels, deliverability and recent objections. Decide the conditions for a draft, without inventing recipients.
- Training/certifications: reported hiring relevance, free/paid status and time cost. Decide whether any learning deserves time during applications.
- Targeted projects: seek actual outcome accounts and compare a new build against adjacent experience, a clearer narrative and authentic referrals. Give build/no-build criteria. If outcome evidence is missing, say so.
- Other tactics: referrals, timing, truthful screening language, LinkedIn consistency and interview evidence. Add only useful supported findings and clearly label operational defaults.

Method:
1. State the run date and rolling thirty-calendar-day window.
2. Find and read the actual installed last30days SKILL.md. Verify its Python requirement and installed interpreter.
3. Run its documented doctor/diagnostics. Distinguish configuration availability, cached evidence and live run status. Never claim an unavailable diagnostic passed.
4. Rewrite each research topic in natural practitioner language. Save the actual query plans and compare planned versus executed questions.
5. Use last30days as the primary engine. Read and preserve source status after every run. A healthy no-results state is different from partial, rate-limited, auth-failed, unreachable, timeout, error and not-attempted.
6. Repair safe local execution issues. If quick mode drops a query, run that query separately. Avoid treating another concurrent run's global cache as this run's evidence.
7. Use dated native-web fallback only for failed pre-flight or failed/degraded source classes. Record the trigger and coverage it supplies. Directly inspect primary sources, verify publication dates, and keep older or undated material as background only.
8. Do not count a fresh crawl, syndicated repost, course catalog refresh or recent article quoting old discussions as recent practitioner evidence.
9. Prefer hiring-side descriptions of actual behavior over candidate claims. Check source role/context and commercial incentives. An adjacent design discussion is not direct proof about AI enablement.
10. Keep conflicting reports visible. Separate interview invitations, offers, acceptances and hires. No anecdote establishes a general conversion rate.

Classification:
- Retain E1 for hiring-side confirmation, multiple independent reports, or reported measurable outcomes under the source definition, while naming the qualifying basis and its limits.
- Retain E2 for unverified candidate anecdotes and practitioner opinion. Where a candidate claims an outcome but verification is absent, do not inflate it to proven effectiveness.
- Treat cost/time catalog information as primary factual background, not hiring-outcome evidence.
- Explicitly label recommended word counts, cadence and effort budgets as operational defaults unless comparative evidence supports them.
- A missing source is a coverage gap. A missing qualifying example in retrieved material is not proof that no example exists.

Deliver:
1. A prioritized playbook covering all seven threads. Each tactic includes linked sources and publication dates, E1/E2 and qualifying basis, candidate applicability, a dated DO/DON'T, and the artifact affected.
2. A gap list stating the cause and effect of every unresolved load-bearing question.
3. One to three first actions for each exact role example, distinguishing preparation priority from eligibility. Keep unverified pay and workplace terms unresolved as explicit dependencies.
4. A per-run coverage report with pre-flight, returned data, exact failures, safe repairs, fallback, and residual limitations.
5. Direct feedback on the process, with a small ordered set of concrete improvements. Critique both prompt design and observed execution behavior.
6. This Markdown prompt changelog containing the full revised prompt, defects, changes, unsolved flags and verdict.

Write new collision-safe Codex-attributed files in this project's docs folder. Preserve original sources. Verify file readback, required sections, source dates, confidentiality and any material contradictions before delivery.

Finally, email the coordinating agent from the authorized connected account. Begin with the requested handoff text naming the runner and the candidate, then state that the work is complete and ready for review. Attach the finished playbook and changelog so the agent can actually use them. Summarize the most important integration dependencies. Do not attach the underlying confidential resume or raw logs.

Read back the sent message to verify recipient, sent state, attachment names and content. Report acceptance/sent verification accurately without claiming the recipient read it. End with the deliverable links, concise findings, email status, and the next action/owner.

## What was wrong and what changed

| Problem | Change |
|---|---|
| “Everything you need is in this prompt” conflicted with missing resume and portfolio contents. | Locate evidence and name conflicts before recommending application claims. |
| Calling four roles “live” encouraged unsupported availability assumptions. | Treat them as dated snapshots. Separate preparation rank from eligibility. |
| E1 combines very different strengths and overlaps E2 for candidate outcome claims. | Name the qualifying basis, retain verification/context limits, keep unverified candidate claims at E2. |
| Recency could be inferred from engine dates or search freshness. | Verify original dates and reject recycled, older, undated and deleted support. |
| Pre-flight could read another run's health data. | Use per-run saved output and inspect topic provenance. |
| Quick mode silently reduced a four-query plan to one. | Check executed query count and rerun omitted questions separately. |
| Engine source breadth could substitute for relevance. | Filter results by reviewer context, actual behavior and role applicability. Preserve broad engine evidence only as provenance. |
| Required exact advice might be invented when evidence is thin. | Label practical word counts, follow-up timing and project caps as judgment under uncertainty. |
| “Email that it is done” might yield a notification without usable research. | Attach the verified report and changelog, then verify the sent message. |

## What rewriting cannot solve

- X authentication failed and Reddit remained partial. The available fallbacks did not recreate full platform coverage.
- Anonymous hiring identities and candidate outcomes cannot be authenticated from these discussions alone.
- The thirty-day window did not produce adequate role-matched evidence for portfolio review duration, targeted-build effectiveness, channel superiority or certification lift.
- Current company terms and role availability were outside scope.
- Candidate-history and master-filter conflicts require reconciliation by the job-search owner and the candidate before submission.
- The shared engine cache and mode behavior are product limitations. This run repaired the lost queries and preserved provenance, but did not modify the installed skill.

**Next action and owner:** the coordinating agent should use the completed playbook within the current initiatives and carry the evidence/input corrections into the next research request.
